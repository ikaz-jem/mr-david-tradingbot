# Build Auditable Strategies Before Automating Trades

Enrivea should launch a **platform-wide, immutable strategy catalog** whose default is `AI decides`, backed by a small set of transparent, bidirectional methods that can be evaluated from closed OHLCV candles. The credible starting set is trend pullback, momentum continuation, range breakout, range mean reversion, volatility expansion, an explicitly defined ICT/Smart Money Concepts interpretation, and an Elliott Wave assistant; volume and multi-timeframe confluence work better as reusable filters than independent strategies. `AI decides` should classify the market regime, run only compatible published strategies, select at most one qualifying method, and return `NO_SETUP` when rules fail or interpretations conflict—it must not invent a technique. **The same strategy identity, immutable version, parameters, evidence, invalidation, and risk policy must follow a result from Research Scanner into Approval Desk and Autopilot.** Administrators should control creation and publication initially; users may save bounded presets, while user-authored rule sets should arrive later as private, research-only strategies that must pass reproducible backtests, held-out tests, and paper observation before becoming eligible for approval or execution. ICT and Elliott Wave are viable only when Enrivea publishes its exact machine interpretation and exposes ambiguity; neither Binance candles nor an AI model can prove institutional intent or identify a uniquely “true” wave count. This approach turns popular public methods into testable product capabilities without implying endorsement, secret knowledge, or guaranteed profitability.

## Seven transparent methods form the credible launch catalog

The product should extract **publicly described analytical principles, not copy a personality’s discretionary calls or claim a famous trader’s returns**. Closed Binance Spot candles provide open, high, low, close, volume, symbol, and interval data; they do not provide historical order-book state, resting stops, funding, open interest, liquidations, wallet flows, news, or trader identity ([Binance Spot market data](https://developers.binance.com/docs/binance-spot-api-docs/rest-api/market-data-endpoints); [Binance Spot glossary](https://developers.binance.com/en/docs/products/spot/faqs/spot_glossary)). That boundary makes trend, momentum, breakout, mean-reversion, volatility, volume, and multi-timeframe logic defensible, while funding-rate, liquidation, true order-flow, and on-chain methods must remain unavailable until their required feeds exist.

| Catalog method | Objective entry evidence | Invalidation and compatible regime | Product treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Trend pullback v1** | Long when `close > EMA20 > EMA50`, EMA20 rises, confirmed swing highs/lows rise, and price closes back with trend after a pullback; inverse for short | Close beyond the last confirmed swing or reversed EMA alignment; reject flat, weakly separated, sideways regimes because moving averages lag and whipsaw ([Binance Academy](https://academy.binance.com/ka-GE/articles/moving-averages-explained); [Fidelity](https://www.fidelity.com/learning-center/trading-investing/technical-analysis/basic-concepts-trend)) | Launch preset; supports long and short research |
| **Momentum continuation v1** | Broader trend alignment plus MACD above its signal with strengthening histogram and RSI in a directionally supportive, non-exhausted range; mirror for short | Momentum reversal, RSI loss of regime midpoint, or structural stop; reject chop because MACD crosses become noisy ([Binance Academy MACD](https://academy.binance.com/ur-PK/articles/macd-indicator-explained/); [Fidelity RSI](https://www.fidelity.com/learning-center/trading-investing/technical-analysis/technical-indicator-guide/RSI)) | Launch preset; expose momentum and regime components |
| **Range breakout v1** | A closed candle beyond the previous N-bar high/low, relative-volume confirmation, and an ATR extension cap; optional retest mode | Close back inside the channel, failed retest, structural stop, or time expiry; a wick alone is not a breakout ([Binance Academy](https://academy.binance.com/ur-PK/glossary/breakout); [TA-Lib](https://ta-lib.org/functions/donchian.html)) | Launch preset; natural successor to the current baseline |
| **Range mean reversion v1** | Only in a detected range: band excursion followed by a close back inside and RSI recovery; mirror for short | New close beyond the excursion extreme plus volatility buffer; prohibit during strong trends because RSI and band extremes can persist ([Binance Academy Bollinger Bands](https://academy.binance.com/lt/articles/bollinger-bands-explained); [Binance Academy RSI](https://academy.binance.com/ur-PK/articles/what-is-the-rsi-indicator)) | Launch preset; strict range gate required |
| **Volatility expansion v1** | Low normalized ATR/Bollinger bandwidth creates a watch state; direction becomes eligible only after a closed, volume-confirmed range break | Failed break, range re-entry, or expiry; compression alone must return no setup because it has no direction ([Fidelity ATR](https://www.fidelity.com/learning-center/trading-investing/technical-analysis/technical-indicator-guide/atr); [Binance Academy](https://academy.binance.com/lt/articles/bollinger-bands-explained)) | Launch preset; useful for alerts and scan queues |
| **Liquidity & imbalance structure v1** | Enrivea’s machine-defined ICT/SMC interpretation: confirmed pivots, equal-high/low clusters, sweep candidate, displacement, structure shift, three-candle imbalance, and premium/discount context | Structural failure, FVG/zone invalidation, or maximum age; never infer stop orders or institutional participation from OHLCV | Launch as research preset after legal naming review |
| **Elliott Wave Assist v1** | Multi-sensitivity pivots generate bounded impulse/ABC candidates; hard wave rules eliminate invalid counts and transparent guidelines rank a preferred count plus alternates | Every count carries a price invalidation; insufficient history, unclear degree, or competing counts return ambiguous/no setup | Research-first preset; not initially eligible for Autopilot |

Volume confirmation should be a shared feature—such as current closed volume divided by an N-period volume average—not its own direction engine, because volume supports participation but does not establish trade direction by itself ([Fidelity](https://www.fidelity.com/learning-center/trading-investing/technical-analysis/what-is-technical-analysis)). Multi-timeframe confluence should similarly derive a higher timeframe from the selected execution timeframe, align only closed candles, and either raise evidence fit or reject conflicting regimes. CME’s educational example deliberately separates broader direction, confirmation, and execution timeframes, supporting a common higher-timeframe filter rather than several disconnected scans ([CME Group](https://www.cmegroup.com/education/courses/using-the-trading-simulator/trading-sim-in-30-seconds-chart-time-frames)). Ichimoku can follow later as a separately versioned trend system; its cloud, Tenkan/Kijun, and lagging-span relationships are codable, but the method also produces more false signals in sideways markets ([Binance Academy](https://academy.binance.com/ur-PK/articles/ichimoku-clouds-explained)).

No public source establishes universally optimal crypto parameters or a durable win rate for these methods. Academic findings on crypto momentum conflict, and indicators remain transformations of historical data rather than predictions ([Borgards, 2021](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1062940821000590); [Grobys and Sapkota, 2019](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0165176519301077)). Defaults are therefore engineering hypotheses that require fee- and slippage-aware validation by symbol, timeframe, direction, and immutable strategy version.

## ICT and Elliott become honest machine interpretations

ICT/Smart Money Concepts should enter the catalog as **Liquidity & Imbalance Structure**, with documentation crediting the public concepts that inspired it and a clear statement that it is Enrivea’s interpretation. ICT’s public material discusses Fair Value Gaps, market-structure shifts, runs on prior highs/lows, and premium/discount around the midpoint of a selected dealing range, but it does not publish one complete machine specification ([Official ICT Episode 6](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bkt8B3kLATQ); [ICT premium/discount transcript](https://pickscribe.com/fr/v/YuefjnUKQdM)). The scanner can objectively detect a bullish three-candle gap when `candle[i-2].high < candle[i].low`, the bearish inverse, fractal pivots confirmed only after right-side bars close, clusters within a declared ATR tolerance, and price moving beyond a selected level then closing back through it. It can also calculate a range midpoint. **It cannot observe stop orders, banks, “smart money,” or institutional intent from OHLCV.** Even ICT’s public order-block explanation says that not every opposite-colored candle is an order block and requires contextual “storyline,” demonstrating why the UI should say “candidate order-block zone” rather than “institutional order confirmed” ([ICT order-block transcript](https://pickscribe.com/v/7WM8qdkanIY)).

Every ICT result should disclose pivot width, lookback, equal-level tolerance, wick-versus-close break policy, displacement threshold, dealing-range selection, minimum imbalance size, mitigation state, and maximum setup age. The deterministic sequence can require an opposing sweep candidate, a displaced close beyond the latest confirmed internal swing, an unmitigated imbalance or candidate order-block zone, and appropriate premium/discount location. A short result is legitimate research, but Spot execution must remain disabled unless the user owns the asset; opening a true short requires a separately approved margin or futures product with its own jurisdiction, leverage, liquidation, and risk controls.

Elliott Wave should launch as **Elliott Wave Assist**, because its hard rules validate a proposed count but do not uniquely discover the correct degree and labeling. Elliott Wave International defines five-wave motive movement and three-wave correction, while its impulse rules require wave 2 to retrace less than 100% of wave 1, wave 3 to extend beyond wave 1 and never be the shortest motive wave, and wave 4 not to enter wave 1 territory for the ordinary cash-market impulse ([EWI Motive Waves](https://www.elliottwave.com/waveopedia/motive-waves/); [EWI Impulse](https://www.elliottwave.com/waveopedia/impulse/)). The same primary source recognizes zigzags, flats, triangles, and combinations; it also states that several valid interpretations commonly coexist and distinguishes a preferred count from alternates ([EWI Corrective Waves](https://www.elliottwave.com/waveopedia/corrective-waves/); [EWI Waveopedia](https://www.elliottwave.com/waveopedia/)).

The engine should therefore extract confirmed pivots at multiple sensitivities, enumerate a bounded set of impulse and ABC candidates, eliminate hard-rule violations, and score survivors by disclosed guidelines such as retracement relationship, alternation, channel fit, momentum, volume, and simplicity. It should show one preferred and up to two alternate counts, each with its own invalidation, and mark incomplete patterns provisional. Fibonacci zones appear only after a valid candidate count exists; they are relationships, not guaranteed reversal prices. **“Pattern fit 78/100” must never become “78% chance of profit.”** When history is insufficient or counts remain too close, ambiguity is the correct product result.

## AI decides means constrained selection, not invention

`AI decides` should be the default option across all three products, but the name must describe a controlled routing process. The engine first validates freshness and candle continuity, classifies the regime as trend, range, compression, high volatility, or ambiguous, and computes all indicators deterministically. It then evaluates every active strategy version compatible with the product, symbol, timeframe, direction, market type, data feeds, and jurisdiction. Hard rules decide eligibility; deterministic component scores rank eligible proposals. AI receives only server-computed, allow-listed evidence and returns a structured selection and explanation. **It may choose one published strategy or `NO_SETUP`; it cannot relax risk rules, manufacture indicator values, combine unnamed methods, or execute a trade.** OpenAI’s safety guidance supports structured fields, fixed schemas/enums, least privilege, and keeping untrusted user content out of higher-authority instructions ([OpenAI agent safety](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/agent-builder-safety)).

Selection should be a shared control, not three separate implementations. A stable `Strategy` identifies the method; an immutable `StrategyVersion` contains its typed rules, parameters, requirements, prompt rubric, invalidation, and risk defaults; a `StrategyPreset` stores a user’s bounded overrides; and a `StrategyAssignment` records whether a product uses an explicit preset or `AI_AUTO`. A common evaluator should emit a normalized `TradeProposal` containing strategy and version IDs, `selectedBy`, normalized parameters, market/regime, direction, eligibility, hard failures, confirmations, entry zone, stop, targets, expiry, evidence dimensions, indicator snapshot, data timestamp, and cache provenance.

Research Scanner displays that proposal and its evidence. Approval Desk adds decision expiry, one-click order review, account balances, venue constraints, and explicit confirmation. Autopilot accepts only a strategy version with a live-approved release and additionally applies the user’s notional, exposure, daily-loss, drawdown, cooldown, and kill-switch policies. A strategy may therefore be available in Scanner while unavailable in Approval Desk or Autopilot. This avoids the dangerous inference that a good-looking research card is automatically execution-ready. Competitor workflows reinforce staged review: Coinrule converts user descriptions into inspectable rules before deployment, while Altrady pre-fills setups for user confirmation ([Coinrule Cloud](https://cloud.coinrule.com/docs); [Altrady](https://www.altrady.com/pricing)).

The user interface should remember a separate assignment for each product but reuse the same catalog and preset IDs. Research Scanner starts with `AI decides` and offers filtered strategy cards showing method, supported directions, required history, compatible timeframes, risk tier, validation class, and limitations. After analysis it reveals the selected method and version even though the underlying OpenAI model remains hidden. Approval Desk and Autopilot show only their eligible subset and explain why a saved preset is unavailable. The existing five-minute shared cache must include `symbol + timeframe + last closed candle + strategyVersion/hash + normalized parameters + risk-policy version + AI policy/prompt version`; an `AI_AUTO` cache record must also preserve the underlying chosen strategy. Otherwise, changing strategies or parameters could incorrectly reuse another method’s result.

Confidence should be decomposed into **data quality, regime fit, rule/pattern fit, trigger quality, volume confirmation, volatility quality, and multi-timeframe alignment**. A high-confidence `NO_SETUP` means strong evidence that mandatory conditions are absent, not a high probability of loss or profit. Outcome probability should remain absent until Enrivea has enough out-of-sample, version-specific observations to calibrate it. The existing semicircle meter can show “evidence fit” after research, but its label and explanation should change with the decision: setup evidence for a qualifying proposal, stay-out evidence for no setup, and pattern fit for Elliott candidates.

## Admin curation precedes safe user creation

The best initial fit is **administrator publication plus user-owned bounded presets**. Administrators can create a draft, clone a built-in, configure allow-listed indicators and rule-tree operations, define long/short logic, set parameter bounds, attach sources and limitations, select compatible products and timeframes, run validation, publish a new immutable version, pause it, deprecate it, or revoke it. Editing a published version always creates a new draft; historical scans, approvals, orders, analytics, and cache records retain the exact old version and evidence. TradingView’s typed inputs demonstrate a familiar pattern of defaults, options, minimums, maximums, and typed values, while QuantConnect keeps parameters external so the same algorithm can be run consistently through testing and deployment ([TradingView inputs](https://www.tradingview.com/pine-script-docs/concepts/inputs/); [QuantConnect parameters](https://www.quantconnect.com/docs/v2/writing-algorithms/optimization/parameters)).

Users should initially be able to select a published method and save private presets containing only fields marked `userAdjustable`, within administrator-defined bounds. This provides meaningful personalization—lookback, approved timeframe, confirmation strictness, risk/reward floor, expiry, and selected filters—without turning a prompt into executable behavior. Arbitrary JavaScript, Python, Pine, Mongo expressions, URLs, model prompts, or system-instruction fragments should never be accepted. Descriptive user text can be stored as low-authority metadata, but the evaluator compiles only a typed rule document composed from allow-listed indicators and operators.

User-created strategies should be phase two, after Enrivea has a reproducible simulator, held-out testing, paper observation, and review queue. New strategies remain private and research-only by default, wear a conspicuous **Custom · unvalidated** badge, and cannot influence platform-level performance statistics. A safe lifecycle is `DRAFT → SCHEMA_VALID → BACKTESTED → OOS_VALIDATED → PAPER → REVIEW → PUBLISHED_RESEARCH → PUBLISHED_APPROVAL → LIVE_CANARY → LIVE`, with pause, failure, deprecation, and revocation states. Freqtrade explicitly advises backtesting and dry-running public strategies rather than treating shared code as production-ready, and Alpaca notes that paper fills remain simulations that can diverge from live markets ([Freqtrade](https://docs.freqtrade.io/en/latest/strategy-customization/); [Alpaca](https://alpaca.markets/support/difference-paper-live-trading)). Historical results must never automatically grant execution permission.

Promotion should be a signed review event that records the strategy hash, evaluator and simulator versions, exact data windows and universe, in-sample and held-out partitions, parameter set, capital, fees, spread/slippage assumptions, trade sample, reviewer, date, and approved products. Validation must test warm-up sufficiency, closed-candle alignment, long/short symmetry, invalidation, conflicting outcomes, causal rolling windows, delayed pivot confirmation, higher-timeframe joins, parameter sensitivity, cost sensitivity, and look-ahead leakage. Freqtrade identifies negative shifts, whole-column aggregations, unsafe resampling, and absolute indexes as common future-data leaks, while TradingView and QuantConnect emphasize selection bias, overfitting, and held-out or walk-forward evaluation ([Freqtrade](https://docs.freqtrade.io/en/latest/strategy-customization/); [TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/pine-script-docs/concepts/strategies/); [QuantConnect](https://www.quantconnect.com/docs/v2/writing-algorithms/optimization/parameters)).

Admin analytics should stay version-specific and separate simulated, paper, approval, and live performance. Each view should show setup frequency, no-setup reasons, accepted/rejected/expired counts, win/loss/flat outcomes, realized and unrealized P&L, average R, expectancy, drawdown, fees, slippage, sample size, date range, assets, directions, and timeframes. CFTC guidance warns that hypothetical results can over- or underestimate performance because assumed fills do not experience actual conditions and that no system can guarantee profit ([CFTC](https://www.cftc.gov/LearnAndProtect/AdvisoriesAndArticles/fraudadv_tradingsystem.html)). Enrivea must therefore forbid “guaranteed,” “risk-free,” “institutional accuracy,” and similar claims, label every performance class beside the metric, and never aggregate materially changed versions into one headline return.

## Conclusion

The strategic advantage is not owning the longest menu of trader names; it is making every method **inspectable, reproducible, versioned, and safely portable from research to execution**. In that system, AI adds value by choosing among valid hypotheses and explaining evidence, while deterministic services retain authority over measurements, eligibility, risk, and orders. That division also creates a durable data asset: Enrivea can learn which immutable method versions work in which regimes without confusing model eloquence, user behavior, and actual trade performance.

Admin curation is the right launch boundary, but the architecture should anticipate a creator ecosystem without prematurely becoming a strategy marketplace. Bounded presets deliver personalization now; later, private no-code strategies can earn broader permissions through evidence rather than popularity. The result is a richer platform that can responsibly support ICT-inspired structure, Elliott alternatives, conventional technical systems, long and short research, and future feeds—without promising knowledge the data cannot contain.
