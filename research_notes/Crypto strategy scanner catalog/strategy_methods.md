# Crypto Strategy Methods for an OHLCV Scanner

## Which strategies can be expressed reliably from closed OHLCV candles?

### Takeaway
Closed OHLCV candles can support transparent, deterministic implementations of trend, momentum, breakout, mean-reversion, volatility, volume-confirmation, and multi-timeframe methods. The product should present these as named analytical rule sets—not as strategies copied from a trader, and never as proven profit systems.

### Cited Findings

#### 1. Trend-following: moving-average alignment and market structure
- Moving averages smooth price, with SMA weighting observations equally and EMA weighting recent prices more heavily; both are lagging indicators and can give false signals, so they should not stand alone. — [Binance Academy: Moving Averages Explained](https://academy.binance.com/ka-GE/articles/moving-averages-explained)
- An uptrend can be defined from ascending peaks and troughs, a downtrend from descending peaks and troughs, and a sideways market from a horizontal range. — [Fidelity: Basic Concepts of Trend](https://www.fidelity.com/learning-center/trading-investing/technical-analysis/basic-concepts-trend)
- **Deterministic candidate:** classify long trend when `close > EMA20 > EMA50`, EMA20 slope is positive, and recent confirmed swing highs/lows are rising; classify short trend using the inverse. A pullback entry can require price to return to EMA20 or the last broken swing and then close back in the trend direction. Invalidate on a close beyond the last confirmed swing or when EMA alignment reverses. This is a product inference from the cited moving-average and peak/trough definitions, not a sourced claim of profitability. — [Binance Academy](https://academy.binance.com/ka-GE/articles/moving-averages-explained); [Fidelity](https://www.fidelity.com/learning-center/trading-investing/technical-analysis/basic-concepts-trend)
- **Regime limit:** moving-average signals lag and may whipsaw in sideways markets; the engine should reject this method when trend separation is small, slopes are flat, or a trend-strength/range filter reports chop. — [Binance Academy](https://academy.binance.com/ka-GE/articles/moving-averages-explained)

#### 2. Momentum continuation: MACD plus RSI regime
- Standard MACD is `EMA12 − EMA26`, with a 9-period EMA signal line; signal-line and zero-line crossovers can express momentum changes, but MACD lags and performs poorly in ranging markets. — [Binance Academy: MACD Indicator](https://academy.binance.com/ur-PK/articles/macd-indicator-explained/)
- RSI measures the speed and magnitude of recent price changes on a 0–100 scale; 14 periods is standard, while 70/30 are conventional overbought/oversold reference levels. Extreme RSI is not itself proof of reversal because strong trends can remain extreme. — [Binance Academy: RSI](https://academy.binance.com/ka-GE/glossary/relative-strength-index)
- Fidelity notes that RSI often occupies different ranges in uptrends and downtrends, which supports using it as a trend-regime measure rather than automatically fading every 70/30 reading. — [Fidelity: RSI](https://www.fidelity.com/learning-center/trading-investing/technical-analysis/technical-indicator-guide/RSI)
- **Deterministic candidate:** long when the broader trend is bullish, MACD is above its signal and histogram is increasing, while RSI is above 50 but below a configurable exhaustion ceiling; short using the inverse. Invalidate when MACD momentum reverses, RSI loses the regime midpoint, or price breaks the structural stop. This is an implementation inference, not a guarantee. — [Binance Academy: MACD](https://academy.binance.com/ur-PK/articles/macd-indicator-explained/); [Binance Academy: RSI](https://academy.binance.com/ka-GE/glossary/relative-strength-index)
- **Regime limit:** disable or heavily penalize this family during range/chop because repeated MACD crosses can be false and shorter/sensitive settings create more noise. — [Binance Academy: MACD](https://academy.binance.com/ur-PK/articles/macd-indicator-explained/)

#### 3. Price-channel breakout: Donchian/range breakout with volume confirmation
- A breakout above resistance or below support suggests a possible trend in the direction of the move; useful confirmations include a candle close beyond the level, higher volume versus the preceding range, and a retest that holds. — [Binance Academy: Breakout](https://academy.binance.com/ur-PK/glossary/breakout)
- Donchian's original four-week concept buys a break above preceding highs and sells a break below preceding lows; the lookback can be generalized into an N-period price channel. — [TA-Lib: Donchian Channels](https://ta-lib.org/functions/donchian.html); [StockCharts: Donchian Guidelines](https://chartschool.stockcharts.com/table-of-contents/overview/donchian-trading-guidelines)
- **Deterministic candidate:** long when a *closed* candle closes above the previous N-bar high, relative volume exceeds a configured threshold, and the breakout candle is not excessively extended in ATR units; short below the previous N-bar low. Optional retest mode waits for the broken boundary to hold. Invalidate on a close back inside the channel, failed retest, structural stop, or time expiry. This combines the cited breakout and channel definitions. — [Binance Academy](https://academy.binance.com/ur-PK/glossary/breakout); [TA-Lib](https://ta-lib.org/functions/donchian.html)
- **Regime limit:** a level touch or wick alone should not count; low-volume breakouts and already overextended candles should return “no setup” because the cited confirmation guidance requires a close and meaningful participation. — [Binance Academy](https://academy.binance.com/ur-PK/glossary/breakout)

#### 4. Mean reversion: Bollinger-band excursion plus momentum recovery
- Standard Bollinger Bands use a 20-period SMA and outer bands two standard deviations away; band contraction measures lower volatility, expansion measures higher volatility, and a band touch is not sufficient on its own to confirm a reversal. — [Binance Academy: Bollinger Bands](https://academy.binance.com/lt/articles/bollinger-bands-explained)
- RSI 70/30 thresholds are conventional but can remain extreme during strong trends, so combining them with trend, volume, and support/resistance filters is recommended. — [Binance Academy: RSI](https://academy.binance.com/ur-PK/articles/what-is-the-rsi-indicator)
- **Deterministic candidate:** only in a detected range, identify a long candidate after a close outside/below the lower band followed by a close back inside while RSI recovers from oversold; short candidate is the mirror image. Use the middle band or opposite side of the range as a target reference and invalidate on a fresh close beyond the excursion extreme plus a volatility buffer. This is a conservative implementation inference from the cited indicators. — [Binance Academy: Bollinger Bands](https://academy.binance.com/lt/articles/bollinger-bands-explained); [Binance Academy: RSI](https://academy.binance.com/ur-PK/articles/what-is-the-rsi-indicator)
- **Regime limit:** prohibit mean-reversion setups during strong directional regimes because RSI and band extremes can persist rather than reverse. — [Binance Academy: RSI](https://academy.binance.com/ka-GE/glossary/relative-strength-index)

#### 5. Volatility compression/expansion
- ATR measures volatility rather than direction; expanding ATR indicates larger ranges, prolonged low ATR can indicate consolidation, and ATR multiples can adapt stops or triggers to current volatility. — [Fidelity: Average True Range](https://www.fidelity.com/learning-center/trading-investing/technical-analysis/technical-indicator-guide/atr)
- A Bollinger Band squeeze indicates contraction and possible future volatility expansion but does not identify direction. — [Binance Academy: Bollinger Bands](https://academy.binance.com/lt/articles/bollinger-bands-explained)
- **Deterministic candidate:** detect compression when normalized ATR and/or Bollinger bandwidth fall below a rolling percentile; do not choose direction until a closed candle breaks the compression range with volume and price confirmation. Stops and extension filters can use ATR. — [Fidelity: ATR](https://www.fidelity.com/learning-center/trading-investing/technical-analysis/technical-indicator-guide/atr); [Binance Academy: Bollinger Bands](https://academy.binance.com/lt/articles/bollinger-bands-explained); [Binance Academy: Breakout](https://academy.binance.com/ur-PK/glossary/breakout)
- **Regime limit:** compression is a watch state, not a directional signal. A scan must return “no setup / compression” until a direction is confirmed. — [Binance Academy: Bollinger Bands](https://academy.binance.com/lt/articles/bollinger-bands-explained)

#### 6. Volume confirmation as a reusable filter
- Volume can confirm price strength: rising volume can support a move, while low volume can indicate weaker participation. — [Fidelity: What Is Technical Analysis?](https://www.fidelity.com/learning-center/trading-investing/technical-analysis/what-is-technical-analysis)
- Accumulation/Distribution combines each close's location within the bar with volume; divergence between price and the indicator can warn that a trend may stall, but the indicator also has known limitations because it ignores close-to-close change. — [Fidelity: Accumulation Distribution](https://www.fidelity.com/learning-center/trading-investing/technical-analysis/technical-indicator-guide/accumulation-distribution)
- **Deterministic candidate:** implement `relativeVolume = currentClosedVolume / SMA(volume, N)` and optionally an Accumulation/Distribution slope/divergence feature. Treat volume as confirmation or a confidence component, not a standalone direction generator. — [Fidelity: Technical Analysis](https://www.fidelity.com/learning-center/trading-investing/technical-analysis/what-is-technical-analysis); [Fidelity: Accumulation Distribution](https://www.fidelity.com/learning-center/trading-investing/technical-analysis/technical-indicator-guide/accumulation-distribution)

#### 7. Multi-timeframe confluence
- CME's educational example uses a daily chart for broad direction, a one-hour chart for confirmation, and a five-minute chart for execution; it emphasizes combining timeframes rather than treating them independently. — [CME Group: Chart Time Frames](https://www.cmegroup.com/education/courses/using-the-trading-simulator/trading-sim-in-30-seconds-chart-time-frames)
- CMT Association describes multi-timeframe analysis as a framework in which daily, weekly, and intraday views help with trend interpretation, timing, and risk management. — [CMT Association: Price, Time, and Volume](https://cmtassociation.org/video/price-time-and-volume-a-unified-approach-to-market-structure/)
- **Deterministic candidate:** derive a higher timeframe automatically from the selected execution timeframe, allow a signal only when the higher-timeframe regime agrees, and compute entries/stops on the selected timeframe. Mixed regimes reduce confidence or return no setup. — [CME Group](https://www.cmegroup.com/education/courses/using-the-trading-simulator/trading-sim-in-30-seconds-chart-time-frames); [CMT Association](https://cmtassociation.org/video/price-time-and-volume-a-unified-approach-to-market-structure/)

#### 8. Composite trend system: Ichimoku (optional later catalog item)
- Ichimoku combines five calculations for trend, momentum, and support/resistance; cited signals include Tenkan/Kijun crosses, cloud breakouts, and Chikou confirmation. Binance notes it tends to work better in trends and can produce more false signals in sideways conditions. — [Binance Academy: Ichimoku Clouds](https://academy.binance.com/ur-PK/articles/ichimoku-clouds-explained)
- **Deterministic candidate:** long only when price is above the cloud, Tenkan is above Kijun, future cloud is bullish, and the lagging span confirms; short with mirrored conditions. Invalidate on a close into/through the cloud or Kijun/structure failure. This should be a separately versioned preset because crypto's 24/7 market makes parameter choice material. — [Binance Academy](https://academy.binance.com/ur-PK/articles/ichimoku-clouds-explained)

### Inferences
- The initial production catalog should prioritize five auditable presets: `trend_pullback_v1`, `momentum_continuation_v1`, `range_breakout_v1`, `range_mean_reversion_v1`, and `volatility_expansion_v1`; `volume_confirmation_v1` and `multi_timeframe_confluence_v1` should be shared filters rather than standalone strategies. These presets map directly to the objective features supported by the cited OHLCV material. — [Fidelity indicator guide](https://www.fidelity.com/learning-center/trading-investing/technical-analysis/technical-indicator-guide/overview); [CME technical-analysis course](https://www.cmegroup.com/education/courses/technical-analysis)
- “AI decides” should first classify the market regime (trend/range/compression/high volatility), then evaluate only compatible deterministic presets, rank qualifying candidates, and explain the selected evidence. AI should not invent indicator values or bypass hard eligibility and risk rules. This architecture follows the cited regime limitations of moving averages, MACD, RSI, Bollinger Bands, and Ichimoku. — [Binance Academy: Moving Averages](https://academy.binance.com/ka-GE/articles/moving-averages-explained); [Binance Academy: MACD](https://academy.binance.com/ur-PK/articles/macd-indicator-explained/); [Binance Academy: Bollinger Bands](https://academy.binance.com/lt/articles/bollinger-bands-explained); [Binance Academy: Ichimoku](https://academy.binance.com/ur-PK/articles/ichimoku-clouds-explained)
- Every strategy definition should be immutable and versioned once used (`strategyKey`, `version`, parameters, source notes, eligible products, status). Admins may create draft strategies from a constrained rule builder; users should initially choose approved presets and save parameter overrides only within administrator-set bounds. This governance inference is necessary because parameter and timeframe choices materially change noise and false-signal behavior. — [Binance Academy: Moving Averages](https://academy.binance.com/ka-GE/articles/moving-averages-explained); [Binance Academy: MACD](https://academy.binance.com/ur-PK/articles/macd-indicator-explained/)

### Gaps
- No authoritative source establishes universally optimal indicator parameters for crypto. Defaults above are starting specifications that require walk-forward testing, fee/slippage modeling, and per-timeframe validation; they must not be marketed as optimal. Conflicting academic evidence also exists: one peer-reviewed study reports dynamic time-series momentum evidence, while another finds insignificant momentum payoffs over 2014–2018. — [Borgards, 2021](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1062940821000590); [Grobys & Sapkota, 2019](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0165176519301077)
- Candles alone cannot prove that a pattern will persist; indicators are transformations of historical price/volume and should be described as evidence, not predictions. — [Fidelity: Technical Analysis transcript](https://www.fidelity.com/bin-public/060_www_fidelity_com/documents/learning-center/Transcript_Technical%20analysis%20in%20ATP.pdf)

## What indicators, confirmations, invalidations, and market-regime limits define each?

### Takeaway
Every catalog strategy should use the same schema: deterministic eligibility, direction, evidence metrics, entry zone, structural/ATR invalidation, target methodology, maximum age, compatible regimes, and explicit “no setup” reasons. Confidence should measure the strength/completeness of evidence for the decision—including a no-setup decision—not the probability of profit.

### Cited Findings
- ATR is non-directional and useful for adaptive triggers/stops, while fixed thresholds ignore changing volatility. — [Fidelity: ATR](https://www.fidelity.com/learning-center/trading-investing/technical-analysis/technical-indicator-guide/atr)
- Breakouts are better represented by a closed candle beyond a level and supported by volume/retest confirmation than by a wick through the level. — [Binance Academy: Breakout](https://academy.binance.com/ur-PK/glossary/breakout)
- Trend lines and trend changes should be confirmed with additional tools rather than treated as certain from a single break. — [Fidelity: Basic Concepts of Trend](https://www.fidelity.com/learning-center/trading-investing/technical-analysis/basic-concepts-trend)
- RSI extremes, MACD crosses, moving-average crosses, and cloud signals can all produce false signals in incompatible regimes. — [Binance Academy: RSI](https://academy.binance.com/ka-GE/glossary/relative-strength-index); [Binance Academy: MACD](https://academy.binance.com/ur-PK/articles/macd-indicator-explained/); [Binance Academy: Moving Averages](https://academy.binance.com/ka-GE/articles/moving-averages-explained); [Binance Academy: Ichimoku](https://academy.binance.com/ur-PK/articles/ichimoku-clouds-explained)
- **Shared specification:** confirmation should require all mandatory rules on closed candles; invalidation should be the nearer of a structural failure and a volatility-aware boundary; time invalidation should expire an untriggered idea after a configured number of candles; regime incompatibility should return no setup before AI narration. This is an engineering inference grounded in the cited confirmation, lag, and volatility limitations. — [Fidelity: ATR](https://www.fidelity.com/learning-center/trading-investing/technical-analysis/technical-indicator-guide/atr); [Binance Academy: Breakout](https://academy.binance.com/ur-PK/glossary/breakout)

### Inferences
- A reusable strategy evaluator should return structured fields: `strategyKey`, `strategyVersion`, `regime`, `direction`, `eligible`, `hardFailures[]`, `confirmations[]`, `entry`, `stop`, `targets[]`, `expiresAt`, `evidenceScore`, and raw indicator snapshots.
- Confidence should be decomposed in the UI into trend/regime fit, trigger quality, volume confirmation, volatility quality, and multi-timeframe alignment. A 70/100 “no setup” means strong evidence that mandatory conditions are absent, not 70% win probability.
- The evaluation order should be: data quality → regime → strategy eligibility → entry/stop/target geometry → minimum reward/risk → stale/duplicate check → AI explanation. A failed hard rule must remain failed regardless of AI language.

### Gaps
- A universal confidence calibration is unavailable. Scores must initially be evidence-completeness scores and labeled accordingly; probability language requires out-of-sample calibration against realized outcomes by strategy, symbol, direction, and timeframe.
- Entry and invalidation parameters must be backtested with Binance fees, spread, slippage, delistings, and survivorship controls before any execution product relies on them.

## Which strategies are unsuitable without derivatives, order-book, on-chain, or interpretive data?

### Takeaway
Funding/open-interest, liquidation, true order-flow, on-chain, sentiment/news, and institution-attribution strategies cannot be honestly produced from Spot OHLCV alone. Elliott Wave and broad ICT/“smart money” labels are also poor initial presets unless narrowed into explicit machine-testable rules with alternate interpretations disclosed.

### Cited Findings
- Binance defines a Spot kline as open, high, low, close, volume and related market data for one symbol and interval; it is not a historical order-book, funding, open-interest, liquidation, or on-chain feed. — [Binance Developer Docs: Spot API Glossary](https://developers.binance.com/en/docs/products/spot/faqs/spot_glossary)
- Binance exposes futures funding and mark/index-price information through separate derivatives endpoints, demonstrating that these are separate datasets from Spot klines. — [Binance Developer Docs: Futures Market Data](https://developers.binance.com/docs/derivatives/coin-margined-futures/market-data/rest-api/Get-Funding-Info)
- Binance's depth stream provides bid/ask price and quantity updates for maintaining a local order book; this cannot be reconstructed from completed OHLCV bars. — [Binance Developer Docs: Spot WebSocket Streams](https://developers.binance.com/zh-CN/docs/products/spot/testnet/web-socket-streams)
- Elliott Wave International states that two or more valid wave interpretations usually exist at a time and analysts choose preferred and alternate counts; it also distinguishes three hard impulse rules from probabilistic guidelines. — [Elliott Wave International: Waveopedia](https://www.elliottwave.com/waveopedia/); [Elliott Wave International: Rules and Guidelines](https://www.elliottwave.com/articles/elevate-your-elliott-wave-analysis/)
- **Unsuitable with current data:** funding-rate mean reversion, open-interest divergence, liquidation cascades/maps, basis trades, long/short positioning, order-book imbalance, footprint/delta, hidden-liquidity claims, on-chain flows, whale-wallet activity, exchange inflow/outflow, news/sentiment/event strategies, and cross-exchange arbitrage. Each requires data not present in a Spot OHLCV kline. — [Binance Spot glossary](https://developers.binance.com/en/docs/products/spot/faqs/spot_glossary); [Binance futures market data](https://developers.binance.com/docs/derivatives/coin-margined-futures/market-data/rest-api/Get-Funding-Info); [Binance depth stream](https://developers.binance.com/zh-CN/docs/products/spot/testnet/web-socket-streams)
- **Elliott Wave status:** feasible as an experimental visualization only after formal swing detection and deterministic wave constraints are implemented; because multiple valid counts can coexist, it should return preferred and alternate interpretations rather than a single authoritative trade. — [Elliott Wave International: Waveopedia](https://www.elliottwave.com/waveopedia/)
- **ICT/Smart Money Concepts status:** do not ship “ICT” as a single strategy. Public material is not a single stable, authoritative mechanical specification, and labels such as order block, liquidity pool, market-structure shift, and fair-value gap require exact definitions before they can be tested. The safe approach is to implement independently named objective primitives—e.g., a three-candle price imbalance, confirmed swing break, prior equal-high/low sweep, displacement threshold—and label them experimental rather than claiming institutional order activity from candles.

### Inferences
- Strategy capability metadata should declare required datasets: `OHLCV`, `ORDER_BOOK`, `TRADES`, `DERIVATIVES`, `ON_CHAIN`, `NEWS`. The scanner must not enable a strategy unless all required feeds are healthy and time-aligned. — [Binance Spot glossary](https://developers.binance.com/en/docs/products/spot/faqs/spot_glossary); [Binance futures market data](https://developers.binance.com/docs/derivatives/coin-margined-futures/market-data/rest-api/Get-Funding-Info)
- The initial admin strategy builder should expose audited primitives rather than free-form executable code or free-form AI prompts. User-created strategies should begin as private saved templates assembled from approved primitives, with limits set by administrators; public/community publishing should wait for moderation, versioning, backtesting, and abuse controls.
- “Top trader strategy” should be avoided as a product label unless licensing and faithful provenance are established. Prefer descriptive method names such as “Range Breakout,” “Trend Pullback,” or “Volatility Expansion.”

### Gaps
- No reliable primary source was found that defines one complete, universally accepted ICT trading algorithm suitable for implementation. It should not be represented as one deterministic or evidence-backed strategy without a separately researched, licensed, versioned specification.
- An Elliott Wave implementation needs a separate design and validation study covering pivot sensitivity, degree selection, alternate counts, repainting, and inter-rater agreement before it can influence automated execution.
- Real short execution is not supported by Spot candles or a Spot-only connection; bearish research can be shown, but execution requires an appropriate margin/futures venue, permissions, symbol rules, leverage controls, and derivatives risk disclosures.

## What minimum risk and disclosure language is appropriate?

### Takeaway
The interface must say that outputs are research based on historical market data, not investment advice or predictions; losses are possible; AI can be wrong; and shorting/leverage can magnify losses. Performance and confidence displays must not imply guaranteed returns or calibrated probabilities unless formally validated.

### Cited Findings
- The CFTC warns that AI cannot predict the future or sudden market changes and that fees, spreads, and subscription costs affect returns. — [CFTC: AI Won't Turn Trading Bots into Money Machines](https://www.cftc.gov/LearnAndProtect/AdvisoriesAndArticles/AITradingBots.html)
- The CFTC states that virtual currencies are more volatile than traditional fiat currencies, leverage amplifies risk, and there is no guaranteed investment or trading strategy. — [CFTC: Understand the Risks of Virtual Currency Trading](https://www.cftc.gov/LearnAndProtect/AdvisoriesAndArticles/understand_risks_of_virtual_currency.html)
- The CFTC advises users not to trade products or strategies they do not understand and to consider only money they can afford to lose. — [CFTC: Virtual Currency Risk Advisory](https://www.cftc.gov/LearnAndProtect/AdvisoriesAndArticles/understand_risks_of_virtual_currency.html)

### Inferences
- **Minimum persistent disclosure:** “For research and educational purposes only—not investment advice. Analyses use historical market data and may be delayed, incomplete, or wrong. AI and technical indicators cannot predict future prices. Crypto assets are volatile and you can lose some or all capital. Short and leveraged positions can amplify losses. Review the market data, fees, spread, slippage, and risks before acting.” — [CFTC AI advisory](https://www.cftc.gov/LearnAndProtect/AdvisoriesAndArticles/AITradingBots.html); [CFTC crypto risk advisory](https://www.cftc.gov/LearnAndProtect/AdvisoriesAndArticles/understand_risks_of_virtual_currency.html)
- **Per-result disclosure:** show data venue, symbol, candle timeframe, last closed-candle timestamp, cache age, strategy/version, whether the result is long/short/research-only, invalidation, and all failed/confirmed rules. Confidence must be labeled “evidence confidence,” with text stating that it is not win probability or expected return.
- **Before execution:** require user confirmation of order side/type/quantity, estimated fees and slippage, stop/target behavior, venue/account, and whether leverage or borrowing applies. Short research should remain non-executable on a Spot-only connection.
- **Performance claims:** separate hypothetical/backtested, paper, and live results; include fees/slippage assumptions and do not use “profitable,” “best,” “institutional,” or win-rate claims without reproducible evidence and legal/compliance review.

### Gaps
- Exact legal wording and required jurisdictional restrictions depend on Enrivea's operating entity, user countries, product execution scope, and whether the service is characterized as research, signals, discretionary management, copy trading, or investment advice. Qualified counsel must review final product copy and flows before launch.
- Appropriate leverage limits, suitability checks, and derivatives disclosures require venue- and jurisdiction-specific review when Futures or margin execution is added.
